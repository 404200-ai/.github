# 404200.ai

### Real-estate SEO first. Deployed systems behind it.

404200.ai is a Cyprus-based company focused first on real-estate SEO for property businesses, developers, architects, and built-environment teams. We combine technical search engineering, useful editorial work, and measurable implementation so strong projects are easier to discover.

We also design and deploy ultra-lightweight websites, web applications, apps, custom business systems, and practical AI automation across Cyprus, the EU, and the MENA region.

## What we do

- **Real-estate SEO:** Technical audits, crawl and indexation work, market and keyword mapping, internal linking, local search, structured data, human-led publishing, and enquiry measurement.
- **Search systems:** Python crawling and analysis with Elixir-based orchestration, including our Spidey operating system for repeatable monitoring and prioritisation.
- **Websites:** Distinctive, fast HTML/CSS builds with technical SEO included from the foundation.
- **Web apps and apps:** Purpose-built interfaces, portals, dashboards, and digital products.
- **Custom systems:** Lightweight software shaped around the way a company actually works.
- **AI and workflow automation:** Readiness assessment, practical roadmaps, implementation, team training, and ongoing support.

## How we work

We start with the commercial question, inspect the technical reality, and deploy useful work. That can mean repairing discovery and indexing, creating a property or project information architecture, publishing evidence-led content, or building the product that the company needs next.

Search outcomes are never sold as guarantees. The work is grounded in clear architecture, consistent execution, first-party knowledge, transparent measurement, and useful experiences for real people.

## Who we work with

We work with property companies, architects, developers, brokers, independent professionals, operations teams, founders, and SMEs that need search visibility or a well-built digital system.
